METHODOLOGY

From problem to practical change.

The execution workflow follows the project methodology: understand the need, build a suitable solution, and evaluate its usefulness.

01

Problem identification

Identify inefficient use or wastage of resources.

02

Idea generation

Invite students to suggest creative, practical ideas.

03

Resource analysis

Review energy, water, waste, materials, and facilities.

04

Technology selection

Choose suitable tools such as web apps, IoT, AI, or sensors.

05

06

Testing & evaluation

Assess cost, efficiency, sustainability, and practical usefulness.

Apply successful ideas, monitor outcomes, and improve continuously.

PROJECT EXECUTION PROCESS

How the idea becomes a working solution.

This step-by-step plan expands the methodology for the Student Innovation project. Each phase should be completed and reviewed before moving to the next.

PHASE 01
🔎

Identify the problem

Observe campus activities and identify where resources are being wasted, are unavailable, or are being used inefficiently.

  • Choose a campus area or activity.
  • Describe the resource problem clearly.
  • Record observations and the people affected.
OutputProblem statement
PHASE 02
💡

Generate ideas

Encourage students to propose possible improvements and compare them for practicality and usefulness.

  • Brainstorm more than one idea.
  • Discuss who benefits from each idea.
  • Shortlist an idea that can be tested.
OutputSelected idea
PHASE 03
📊

Analyze resources

Understand the current resource situation before deciding what to change.

  • Check energy and electricity use.
  • Review water, waste, and materials.
  • Consider campus facilities and constraints.
OutputResource analysis
PHASE 04
🧩

Select technology

Choose technology based on the problem, the data needed, and the level of monitoring required.

  • Consider IoT and sensors for resource data.
  • Consider AI for analysis where appropriate.
  • Consider a mobile app or web interface.
OutputTechnology plan
PHASE 05
🛠️

Develop the solution

Build a prototype that translates the selected idea into a practical workflow or digital solution.

  • Define inputs and expected outputs.
  • Build the selected functions.
  • Show resource information clearly to users.
OutputWorking prototype
PHASE 06
🧪

Test and evaluate

Check whether the prototype works and whether it addresses the original resource problem.

  • Test normal and unexpected inputs.
  • Review usability and reliability.
  • Evaluate efficiency, cost, and sustainability.
OutputTest findings
PHASE 07
🌱

Implement and improve

Use successful findings to guide implementation, monitor results, and gather feedback for further improvement.

  • Introduce the solution in the chosen setting.
  • Monitor resource use and user feedback.
  • Refine the solution based on findings.
OutputImproved solution
↻

Continuous improvement loop — feedback and monitoring can reveal new problems, so the process may return to problem identification and repeat.

SYSTEM DESIGN

From resource data to action.

The project design is organized into ten connected layers. Together, they describe how information can move through a resource-monitoring and innovation system.

01

Input

Enter or collect the information needed to understand a resource-related situation.

↓
02

Resource data

Organize information about energy, water, waste, materials, or campus facilities.

↓
03

Processing

Prepare the collected information so it can be reviewed consistently.

↓
04

Analysis

Look for unusual usage, inefficiencies, or opportunities to improve resource utilization.

↓
05

Innovation / solution

Translate the identified need into a practical student-driven idea or prototype.

↓
06

Decision-making

Use the available findings to decide which action or improvement to take.

↓
07

Monitoring

Observe resource use and the performance of the proposed solution over time.

↓
08

Alert

Provide a notification when a defined issue or condition needs attention, if supported by the implementation.

↓
09

User

Present information and actions to students, campus users, or responsible staff.

↓
10

Feedback

Collect feedback and observations to improve the solution and inform the next cycle.

Intelligent use of resources. Lasting impact.

✓ Improve energy and resource efficiency
✓ Reduce avoidable operating costs
✓ Encourage student-led innovation

✓ Use feedback for continuous improvement
